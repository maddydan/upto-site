UPTO · HELP AND INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Updated October 16, 2026 · Operated by Upto

Upto helps you make plans with friends. This policy explains what information Upto handles, who can see it, and how to ask us about it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, username, password, and time zone. We use your password to authenticate you through our account provider; Upto does not store it as readable text. You can add a profile photo, bio, and interests.

We store the plans, comments, responses, friend and group connections, blocks, and reports you create. A plan can include a place you type, but Upto does not request your device's precise location. If you enable phone notifications, we store a device push token and notification preferences. We also process basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as authentication and service logs.

How we use information

We use this information to run your account, show plans to the people allowed by each plan's visibility setting, connect friends, send notifications you enable, prevent abuse, review reports and profile photos, answer support requests, and keep the service secure. We do not use your information for advertising or sell it.

Who can see it and our service providers

Other Upto users may see your name, username, approved photo, bio, interests, and content according to the app's visibility and friendship rules. People included in a plan may see its details and related comments. You should avoid putting sensitive information in a plan or comment.

We use Supabase for accounts, database, and photo storage, and Expo for optional push delivery. These providers process information on our behalf to provide the service. Your phone's notification service also handles push delivery. We may disclose information when legally required or needed to protect users and the service.

Reports and retention

Only authorized Upto reviewers can access safety reports. A report may preserve a copy of the reported text, its author, and the details supplied by the reporter so we can investigate even if the original content changes. We keep report records while needed for safety review, abuse prevention, or legal obligations.

We keep account and content data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under You → Delete account. This removes your profile, plans, comments, connections, and profile photo from the active service. Safety report records, support emails, and information in provider backups or logs may remain for the limited purposes above until they are no longer needed.

Your choices and contact

You can edit your profile, manage blocks, turn off phone notifications, and delete your account in the app. To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of other information, email support@upto.us.com. We may need to verify that the request is yours before acting on it.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above shows the latest version. If a material change affects how we use your information, we will provide notice in the app or by another appropriate method.

Contact us

For help, privacy requests, or safety concerns, email support@upto.us.com.
