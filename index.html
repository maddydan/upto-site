Upto Support

Contact Upto

Need help with Upto? Email camada1781@gmail.com and include your username and a short description. Do not send your password.

Account access

Upto currently uses a username and password. Password reset is not yet available in the app. If you cannot sign in, email us from a safe address and describe the issue. We may ask for information to verify the account before helping.

Safety and reports

Open a plan, comment, or profile and use Report to flag harmful content. You can also block a person from their profile. For another safety concern, email camada1781@gmail.com. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Delete your account

In the app, open You → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. If you cannot access your account, email camada1781@gmail.com to request deletion. We may need to verify that the account is yours. See the Privacy Policy for what may be retained for safety or legal reasons.

Contact us

For help, privacy requests, or safety concerns, email camada1781@gmail.com.
