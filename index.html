UPTO · HELP AND INFORMATION

Upto Support

Contact Upto

Need help with Upto? Email support@upto.us.com and include your username and a short description. Do not send your password.

Account access

New accounts use a verified phone number and password. On the login screen, choose Forgot password? to get a text message code and set a new password. Earlier accounts can still log in with an email or username and password; while signed in, open You → Recovery phone to add a number for future resets. Older accounts with an email can still request an email reset link. If you have an older account with no verified recovery method and cannot sign in, email us from a safe address. We may ask for information to verify the account before helping.

Safety and reports

Open a plan, comment, or profile and use Report to flag harmful content. You can also block a person from their profile. For another safety concern, email support@upto.us.com. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Delete your account

In the app, open You → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. If you cannot access your account, email support@upto.us.com to request deletion. We may need to verify that the account is yours. See the Privacy Policy for what may be retained for safety or legal reasons.

Contact us

For help, privacy requests, or safety concerns, email support@upto.us.com.
